The yoke is worked top down in the round with increases spread across four plain rounds
between each patterned band, so the colourwork sits on an unshaped row every time. This
is fiddlier to write than it is to work.

Short rows across the back neck are what stop the front riding up. They are optional in
the sense that the pullover will exist without them, and not optional in the sense that
it will fit.
