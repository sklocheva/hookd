The raglan yarn. Warmer than sheep wool for the same weight, sheds only lightly, and barely
pills — and it sags badly enough that everything else has to be designed around it. Sleeves
are the worst of it: they end up shapeless sacks unless you work them several centimetres
shorter than the target and let them find their own length.

It blurs stitch definition, so anything that needs the row below to read clearly is a poor
match, and heavy texture only adds sag. It frogs badly. Fold the finished garment, never hang
it. Not superwash, so it felts.

The thing I keep coming back to: it is roughly 24 micron on average and it still itches my
boyfriend. Average fineness is not what causes prickle.
