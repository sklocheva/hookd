The one alpaca-containing yarn that has passed the itch test for me! My boyfriend also wore a beanie
in this and did not react at all.

This alpaca is blown into a hollow tube of organic cotton. Most blown yarns have a nylon core, so a cotton one is a really nice touch. I did not observe any stretch on the cotton at all, maybe because of it's light texture. It does make me think why other yarn brands chose the nylon core if it is not for stability.

It is really hard to frog, I had to scrap a few projects because I could not frog even two rows back!
