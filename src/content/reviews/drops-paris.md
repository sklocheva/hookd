The yarn I have most of, by a wide margin, and the one I reach for when something has to
photograph well. Matte, no halo, and the crispest stitch definition of anything in the
basket — nothing else here films as clearly.

It is hard work in the hand. The twist is loose enough that the strands separate under the
hook, and it gets worse as your own twist accumulates through a session. It also presses into
the tension finger harder than anything else I have used, cheap acrylic included, so I plan it
as short sessions rather than long ones. A smoother hook helps more than slowing down does.

Heavy, with no recovery — a garment in this will grow and stay grown. The finished fabric is
much better than the working experience, which is the reason I keep buying it.
